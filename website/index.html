How it works

Everything fits
in four tabs.

Marketplace, Gigs, Messages, and Wallet — one campus-native app, no clutter.

Marketplace

Buy, sell, and rent peer-to-peer within your verified campus. Clothing rental is the flagship hook — rent formalwear or a one-off outfit instead of buying, then return it. It's why students keep opening the app between big purchase cycles.

Clothes Furniture Textbooks Books Electronics Accessories Household

Gigs

The student-to-student task and service marketplace — a peer labor economy on campus. Post work, pick up work, get paid. Specific service categories are still being finalized as we shape the platform with our earliest campuses.

Messages

In-app chat between buyer and seller, or client and worker. Every confirmed deal or gig gets a confirmation card with a map pin, address, date, and time — tap to open turn-by-turn directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps. Meetups stop being a logistics guessing game.

Wallet & Profile

Payment handling, transaction history, and subscription status, alongside your profile — photo, major, graduation year, and classes — all in one place.
