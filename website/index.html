Why it works

Craigslist energy, retired.

Random group chats, marketplace strangers, "meet me at the gas station" — that era is over. Campus Plus+ keeps every deal inside a network where identity is already settled.

.edu or it doesn't happen

Every account is verified against a real school email before it can buy, sell, or post a gig. If someone's on Campus Plus+, they're a student at your school. Full stop.

Your campus is the whole market

Listings, gigs, and chats stay inside your school's network. No shipping, no flaky commuters from across town — the buyer for your couch lives ten minutes away.

Meetups with a plan

Every confirmed deal generates a card with a pinned address, date, and time. One tap opens turn-by-turn directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps — no logistics guessing game.

Pricing that isn't hidden

Small fees. Smaller with Campus+.

Both sides of every deal pay a little — that's what keeps the network verified, the payments protected, and the lights on. Here's every number.

Free

Standard
Marketplace4% + 4% Buyer and seller each pay 4%, capped at $20 per side.
Gigs5% + 5% Client and worker each pay 5% — 10% total, no cap.

With Campus+

Member
Marketplace2.5% + 2.5% Each side pays 2.5%, capped at $15 per side.
Gigs3.5% + 3.5% 7% total instead of 10% — no cap.

Real math: sell a $300 couch. On the free plan your side of the fee is $12. With Campus+ it's $7.50 — and the more you buy, sell, and gig, the faster the membership pays for itself.

The Founder Program

Four cumulative tiers, earned by the number of verified students your referral code brings in. Every tier keeps the perks of the one before it.
