18. Rental Terms

Applies to any listing marked as a rental or loan, including dress rental, rather than a sale.

18.1 Rental Agreement Formation

A rental agreement is formed when the renter pays through the app for a specified rental period and the lender confirms the meetup for handoff. The confirmation card (rental item, dates, price, meetup details) constitutes the agreed rental terms between the two users.

18.2 Rental Period

The rental period begins at the confirmed pickup time and ends at the confirmed return time shown on the confirmation card. Both parties are responsible for coordinating pickup and return through Messages.

18.3 Condition & Damage

The renter is responsible for returning the item in the same condition it was received, normal wear excepted. For damage, staining, or loss, the renter is responsible for reasonable repair or cleaning costs, or, if the item is lost or damaged beyond reasonable repair, its fair market value as reasonably documented by the lender. A refundable deposit held via Stripe at booking may apply to a given rental listing; deposit amounts and thresholds are being finalized and will be published here once set. Lenders are encouraged to photograph an item's condition before handoff, and renters are encouraged to do the same at pickup and return.

18.4 Late Returns

Items not returned by the confirmed end time may incur a late fee, payable by the renter to the lender (fee amount to be confirmed and published here). Repeated late returns may result in suspension of rental privileges.
