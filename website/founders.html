Referral-driven programs for the students helping Campus Plus+ grow, campus by campus. Everything here — including how arrangements can end — is stated up front.

Campus+ Subscription

Campus+ is the paid membership tier (not the app name) — the value is purely fee reduction, so you keep more on every buy, sell, or gig.

Referral Codes

Your referral code follows a simple format: school abbreviation + first name — for example, uscjake. If your name is already taken at your school, a trailing number is added.
