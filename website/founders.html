Campus+ grows one school at a time — and the students who bring it there first get rewarded for it. Two tracks, run side by side: the referral-driven Founder Program, and the volume-based Campus Ambassador Program.

Founder Program

Four tiers.
Cumulative, public, yours to unlock.

The Founder Program is referral-driven — every verified student you bring onto Campus+ at your school moves you toward the next tier. Tiers are cumulative, so progress never resets.

01

Launch Founder

Your entry point into the program. Share your referral code, verify your first wave of students, and start building toward the next tier.

02

Revenue Share Partner

Referral volume earns you a share of the revenue Campus+ generates from the students you brought in.

03

Campus Founder

Recognized as a founding driver of Campus+ at your school, with the standing and upside that comes with it.

04

Elite Campus Founder

The top of the program — reserved for the students whose referrals defined Campus+ at their campus.

Exact referral thresholds for each tier are confirmed when you apply and can vary by campus as the program scales. Apply to see where you'd start.

Referral codes

Your code is just your school and your name.

Referral codes follow a simple format: school abbreviation + first name — for example, uscjake. If your name's already taken at your school, a trailing number gets added automatically.

Example

uscjake

USC student named Jake. A second Jake at USC would get uscjake2, and so on.

Campus Ambassador Program

Paid on the transactions
you help drive.

Separate from the Founder Program, the Ambassador Program pays a flat monthly bonus based on the transaction volume you drive at your campus. The more activity you generate, the higher your bracket.
