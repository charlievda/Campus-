This Cookie Policy explains how Campus Marketplace LLC ("Campus+," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on joincampusplus.app (the "Site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They let a site remember information about your visit, like preferences and how you interact with the page.

2. How we use cookies

Essential cookies

Required for the Site to function — for example, remembering your session and basic preferences. These can't be switched off.

Analytics cookies

Help us understand how visitors use the Site (pages viewed, time on page, general traffic patterns) so we can improve it. We use aggregated, non-identifying data wherever possible.

Referral tracking

If you arrive at the Site via a Campus+ referral code or Founder/Ambassador link, we may use a cookie to attribute your visit to the correct referrer.

We do not use cookies for third-party advertising on joincampusplus.app.

3. Your choices

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the Site functions. You can also opt out of analytics tracking where we provide an in-page control, if applicable.

4. Cookies in the app

The Campus+ iOS app does not use browser cookies; it uses standard mobile device identifiers and local storage for the same purposes (keeping you signed in, remembering preferences, and basic analytics). See our Privacy Policy for details on app data collection.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
