From account creation to payout, every part of Campus+ is designed to protect students on both sides of every transaction.

How We Protect You

Six layers of protection built into every booking.

01

.edu Email Verification

Every single account on Campus+ requires a valid .edu email address to sign up. This creates a verified-student-only community — no outsiders, no anonymous users.

02

Protected Escrow Payments

When you pay for a booking, your money is held securely in escrow — powered by Stripe. It only releases to the earner once you confirm the job is complete.

03

Two-Sided Review System

After every job, both the buyer and earner leave a review — visible on both profiles. This mutual accountability keeps standards high and bad actors visible.

04

In-App Messaging Only

All communication stays inside Campus+. You never have to share your personal phone number or email. Messages are on-platform, giving you a record and added safety.

05

Block & Report Tools

If something feels off, you can block any user and report them directly from their profile or from a conversation. Reports are reviewed by our team promptly.

06

Campus-Only Network

Your campus network is yours. By building within verified campus communities, we create natural accountability — these are people you might sit next to in class.

18+ Requirement

Campus+ is for adults.

All users must be 18 or older to create an account. Campus+ is a real financial platform handling real payments — we take this requirement seriously and enforce it at signup.

  • Must be 18+ to create an account and transact on Campus+
  • Age is confirmed during identity verification for payouts
  • Accounts found to violate this policy are permanently removed
