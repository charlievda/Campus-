Getting Started

Campus+ is a .edu-verified student-to-student marketplace and gig platform. Every account on Campus+ is a verified student at your school, so buying, selling, renting, and hiring stays in-network instead of open to the whole internet.

USC is our flagship market. TCU, Utah, Georgia, and UCSB are active pilot schools. Campus+ is live or pre-order-ready across 179+ campuses total, with more opening on a rolling basis. See the full status on the Campuses section of the homepage.

Campus+ is currently iOS-only.

Verification

During signup, you verify your school email address, then set up your profile with a photo, major, and graduation year. Only verified students at your school can see and interact with your listings and gigs.

Campus+ is built exclusively for verified college students, so a valid school email is required to create an account.

We collect what's needed to verify you're a real student and to run the marketplace safely. See our Privacy Policy for the full breakdown of what's collected and how it's used.

Marketplace

Clothes, furniture, textbooks, books, electronics, accessories, and household items — bought, sold, or rented peer-to-peer within your verified campus.

List formalwear or a one-off outfit for rent instead of selling it outright. The renter picks it up, wears it, and returns it by the agreed date — coordinated right in Messages, with a confirmation card for the handoff.

Sellers and renters set their own prices on every listing.

Gigs

Gigs is the student-to-student task and service marketplace on campus — a peer labor economy. We're still finalizing the specific service categories with our earliest campuses; check back as this fills out.

Payment runs through your in-app Wallet, alongside your full transaction history.

Fees & Campus+ Pro

Free accounts pay 4% buyer + 4% seller, capped at $20 per side. Campus+ Pro subscribers pay 2.5% buyer + 2.5% seller, capped at $15 per side.

Free accounts pay 5% client + 5% worker (10% total), with no cap. Campus+ Pro subscribers pay 3.5% client + 3.5% worker (7% total), also with no cap.

Lower fees — that's it. Campus+ Pro doesn't gate any features; every account gets full access to Marketplace, Gigs, Messages, and Wallet. Pro just reduces the cut on every transaction.

Meetups & Safety

Once a deal or gig is confirmed in Messages, you'll get a confirmation card with a map pin, address, date, and time. Tap it to open turn-by-turn directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps — no back-and-forth guessing where or when to meet.

Every account is a verified student at your school, which is Campus+'s core safety layer. If something goes wrong, contact support and we'll help sort it out.

Founder & Ambassador Programs

The Founder Program is referral-driven: bringing verified students onto Campus+ moves you through four cumulative tiers. The Ambassador Program is separate, and pays a flat monthly bonus based on the transaction volume you help drive at your campus. Full details are on the Founder Program page.
