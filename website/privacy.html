This Privacy Policy explains how Campus Marketplace LLC ("Campus+," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Campus+ mobile application and joincampusplus.app (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information we collect

Account & verification information

  • Your school (.edu) email address, used to verify student status
  • Name, profile photo, major, and graduation year
  • Class information you choose to add to your profile

Marketplace & Gigs activity

  • Listings you create (items, rentals, gigs), including photos and descriptions
  • Messages exchanged with other users to coordinate deals and gigs
  • Transaction history, including items bought, sold, rented, or gigs completed

Location information

  • Meetup addresses you or another user attach to a confirmation card, so both parties can navigate to the agreed pickup/drop-off point via Apple Maps or Google Maps

Payment & subscription information

  • Transaction and payout details processed through our payment provider(s)
  • Campus+ Pro subscription status, billing period, and renewal date (billing itself is handled by the Apple App Store)

Referral & program information

  • Referral codes you use or generate, and Founder Program / Campus Ambassador Program status and activity, if you participate

Device & usage information

  • Device identifiers, app version, crash logs, and general usage analytics

2. How we use information

  • To verify you're a real student and keep the marketplace campus-only
  • To operate Marketplace, Gigs, Messages, and Wallet, including facilitating transactions and payouts
  • To generate confirmation cards and map directions for meetups
  • To calculate and display Founder Program tiers, Ambassador Program bonuses, and per-campus Pro subscriber counts
  • To bill and manage Campus+ Pro subscriptions
  • To provide customer support and investigate reported problems
  • To maintain the safety, security, and integrity of the Service
  • To improve the app and communicate updates to you

3. How information is shared

  • With other verified students — your profile, listings, and messages are visible to the users you interact with, and to your school's verified community where relevant
  • With service providers — payment processing, cloud hosting, analytics, and customer support tools that help us operate the Service
  • For legal reasons — if required to comply with law, enforce our Terms, or protect the rights, safety, and property of Campus+, our users, or others
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices

  • You can update your profile information in-app at any time
  • You can manage or cancel your Campus+ Pro subscription through your Apple App Store account settings
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing support@joincampusplus.app

5. California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of certain sharing. To exercise these rights, contact support@joincampusplus.app.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Transaction records may be retained longer where required for legal, tax, or safety purposes.

7. Children's privacy

Campus+ is intended for college students verified via a valid school email and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above, and where appropriate, communicated in-app.
