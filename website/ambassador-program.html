Founder Program

Four cumulative tiers, unlocked as the number of students you refer grows — each tier keeps the perks of the one before it.

  1. Launch Founder

    Your entry point into the program. Start referring students at your school and begin building toward the next tier.

  2. Revenue Share Partner

    Unlock an ongoing share of platform revenue generated by the students you've referred.

  3. Campus Founder

    Recognized as a lead Founder for your campus, with added visibility and perks.

  4. Elite Campus Founder

    The highest tier, reserved for the students driving the most referrals and growth at their school.

Exact referral thresholds for each tier are being finalized — check back soon for the full breakdown.

Referral Codes

Your referral code follows a simple format: school abbreviation + first name — for example, uscjake. If your name is already taken at your school, a trailing number is added.
